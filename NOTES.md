Newest first. One entry per commit: date, version/build, what changed and why, and how it was checked.

- 2026-11-27: 1.0.0 build 1, the whole first version (branch claude/barometer-1.0.0, PR, not merged; nothing built with EAS).
  - Why this app: keyword research found barometer at 893 US searches a day, altimeter 698 (altimeter free 1,015), elevation 749, barometric pressure 640, my elevation 360, pressure 308, hpa 213. Three apps in the niche earn, one on about 5,000 downloads; apps 122, 194 and 297 days old sit in the top 10 for barometer. The ones that earn sell pressure history and change alerts, so the app's job is to show the sensor clearly and remember it.
  - Background readings, checked first: expo-sensors reads CMAltimeter only while JavaScript listens; expo-background-task (SDK 57) only submits a BGProcessingTaskRequest with requiresNetworkConnectivity and a 12 hour default, which iOS runs about once a night when idle. Not enough for a history or a 3 hour alert. A small local Swift module (modules/baro-background, like Image to Text's vision-ocr) registers a BGAppRefreshTask and a BGProcessingTask without the network requirement; each run takes one CMAltimeter reading in Swift, keeps it for the app, checks the alert and posts it. iOS decides how often (often a few times a day for an app opened most days; never after a swipe-away, with Background App Refresh off or in Low Power Mode), and the app says so wherever it matters.
  - Engine on main (first commit, unchanged files from cashcounter main at 84c3ba6): index.ts, purchases.ts, state.tsx with the foreground Pro re-check, trial.ts, reminder.ts, reviews.ts, offer.ts, testing.ts, the SQLite layer pattern, the PDF writer and fontkit measuring, the share and print code, the onboarding trial screens, the paywall with three rows, Settings with the hidden test menu, the EAS workflows, plugins/withoutPushEntitlement.js and AGENTS.md. ## TestFlight checklist (1.0.0 build 1)

1. Fresh install: three intro screens; on the first, iOS asks for Motion & Fitness (allow) and the dial shows the live reading. Notifications screen: Allow notifications. Trial and reminder screens if the Apple ID can get the trial, then the paywall: weekly first and preselected with the trial from the store, yearly and lifetime, X after 3 seconds. Close it: "The barometer is free." then the gauge.
2. Read the gauge: a reading near your local weather service's station pressure (not its sea level number). After an hour open, a trend line; after three, "in 3 hours".
3. Change units: Settings, every pressure unit, then feet and meters. The dial, the number, the chart and History follow.
4. Altimeter: tap Reset here at the bottom of stairs or in an elevator, go up two or three floors: the climb shows about 3 m (10 ft) a floor; come back down to about 0. Tap Use my location (allow While Using): a starting elevation and "From location at ...". Deny it on a second phone: the tab still works and says so.
5. Sea level: on the gauge, Sea level; it says which elevation it uses. Settings, Type my elevation (Pro): the gauge says "you typed".
6. Background: open the app, then press the side button and leave it (do not swipe it away) for three or four hours with Background App Refresh on. Open History, 24H: background readings as dots or short lines in the gap, and the gauge's "from the background today" count. Note how many arrived; that is the real rate on your phone. Test menu (seven taps on the version line), Take a background reading now, runs the same Swift path by hand.
7. Set an alert: History, bell, turn on (paywall on a free Apple ID; then with the sandbox purchase), 2 hPa. Ride a lift or drive up a hill of 20 to 30 m and stay there: "Pressure fell ... in the last 3 hours." arrives while open; it must not arrive twice for the same change. More than 15 hPa (a plane) must not alert.
8. Add a note: the tag on the gauge, Headache, Save note: it shows on the chart as 1 and in the list with the pressure and trend. A second note opens the paywall on a free Apple ID. Hold a note to delete it.
9. Export: History, share icon. 7 days, Share PDF (paywall on free), Print, Share CSV. Open the PDF: one page, gaps empty, notes numbered. Open the CSV in Numbers.
10. Hit both paywalls: the onboarding one (test menu, Show onboarding again) and the in-app one from 7D, the alert, a second note, export, a typed elevation, the Marine dial and Settings. Restore purchases on a paywall and in Settings.
11. Dark mode, the largest Dynamic Type size, VoiceOver on the gauge (it reads the number, unit and trend), the chart and the tabs.
12. Low Power Mode on for a few hours: no background readings; the alert screen says Background App Refresh is restricted.

## Noticed, not changed

- The brief's third onboarding line was "get a heads-up before a big change". "Before" reads like a forecast; the alert fires during a change, from stored readings. The screen says "Get a heads-up on a big change." (the description's own wording). Say if it should be "before".
- The Spanish (Mexico) keyword list as briefed was 107 bytes; `hpa` and `inhg` were dropped (already indexed from the English listing) to make 98. Seven more of its words repeat the English keywords; new words there would widen the reach.
- The English keyword field carries headache, migraine and sinus, as briefed. They are invisible keywords, not claims, and the app never makes a health claim; flagging only because the rules keep health words out of visible text.
- The free plan gets one note (shown on the chart) so the feature can be tried; the brief lists "notes on the chart" as Pro and the paywall "on a second note". Say if the free note should stay off the chart.
- A Pro subscription that lapses while the app stays closed keeps the background alert on until the next launch (the module learns the plan from the app).
- Carrying the phone up or down moves the station reading (about 1 hPa per 8 m), so the trend and the chart mix weather and stairs. The steady band (1 hPa) and the 15 hPa alert cap soften it; a phone that travels a lot will show it. Sea level mode holds the elevation fixed, like a wall barometer.
- CMAltimeter needs Motion & Fitness access. If it is denied, the app shows a plain screen with Open Settings, and background readings stop too.
- The renders come from a react-native-web copy; the iOS switch, the share and print sheets, the Motion & Fitness and location prompts look different on a phone, and the dial's text uses the web font renderer.
- The website's App Store button points at apps.apple.com until the app id exists; the JSON-LD has no downloadUrl yet.
- The Swift module cannot be compiled here (no Xcode on Linux). It follows the SDK 57 patterns from expo-background-task and Image to Text's module, but the first EAS build is its first compile.
