Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy Barometer Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Pressure readings, notes, the elevation you set and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the app makes no network calls of its own; nothing is uploaded. A PDF chart or a CSV file leaves your phone only when you share, print or save it.

Permissions

Motion & Fitness. iOS gives apps the pressure sensor through Motion & Fitness. Barometer reads only the air pressure.

Location, while using the app. Asked only on the Altimeter tab when you tap Use my location, to get a starting elevation once. Never in the background. The elevation is kept on your iPhone; your position is not stored or sent anywhere. The app works with location off.

Notifications. Used only for the pressure change alert you turn on, and one reminder the day before a free trial ends. They are made on your iPhone; none come from a server.

Background App Refresh. Lets iOS wake the app now and then to store one pressure reading and check your alert. It does not use location.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.
